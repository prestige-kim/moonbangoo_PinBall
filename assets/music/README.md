# Music licensing

Four recordings by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
Public credits and per-track source links: [music-credits.html](../../music-credits.html).

- Chipper Doodle v2: intro/setup, looping.
- Electrodoodle: mixing/flight, looping.
- Bit Quest: running, looping.
- Winner Winner!: confirmed winners only, first 12 seconds, one-shot.

All files: MP3 96 kbps, 44.1 kHz, 0.3-second fade-in. Result cue also has a 2-second fade-out.
Runtime plays only one background track at a time: the outgoing track fades out over 0.18 seconds and stops before the next starts with a 0.36–0.5-second smoothstep fade-in. Buffering does not keep another song playing underneath. Race music remains until result reveal. Cached media players and Web Audio gains are reused, with at most four tracks. Volume and tab visibility control all players together. Original compositions remain by Kevin MacLeod; conversion/editing and synchronization were done for this game.

`licenses/manifest.json` records source URLs, ISRC, original download hashes, deployed hashes and changes.
`licenses/CC-BY-4.0.html` and `licenses/composer-faq.html` preserve official license and attribution guidance as retrieved on 2026-10-13.
Source HTML receipts contain the official page/player shell; the licensing statement was verified in the rendered official track pages.
No DRM or restrictions are added to these music assets.
